# Audience

## Audience

Film lovers in households of two to five, who share an account and watch across devices.

## Personas

### The commuter

Watches on a phone on the train, often underground, and finishes the film on the TV at home.

### The family evening

Five profiles on one account, one TV, and a child profile that must never see a 12+ film.
