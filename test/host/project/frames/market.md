# Market

## Landscape

The large services compete on volume. Reelhouse competes on a catalogue chosen by editors.

## Competitors

- The large general services.
- Two smaller film-only services.

## Positioning & Differentiators

Every film is chosen, and every collection says why.

## Who it is for and against

For people who finish the films they start; not for background watching.

## Earning Capabilities

Monthly and yearly plans, with a free first week.
