# Profiles

## Intent

An account is usually a household. Each person in it needs their own history, watchlist, and progress, or the recommendations and the Continue watching row stop making sense to anyone.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** An account holds up to five profiles, each with a name and an avatar.
- **REQ2:** A profile marked for younger viewers sees no film rated 12 or above.

## Out of Scope

- Separate passwords per profile.
