# Sign In

## Intent

A viewer signs in once per device and stays signed in. Typing a password on a TV remote is the worst moment of the product, so the TV shows a code instead.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** A viewer signs in on the phone or the web with a link sent to their email.
- **REQ2:** The TV shows a six-letter code the viewer enters on a phone that is signed in.

## Out of Scope

- Signing in with a social account.
