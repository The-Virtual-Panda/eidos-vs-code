# Watchlist

## Intent

A viewer hears about a film on the way home and wants it waiting for them on the TV that evening. The watchlist is that promise: saved once, there on every device.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** A film saved on any device is on the watchlist of the same profile everywhere within a minute.
- **REQ2:** The watchlist lists films newest first.

## Out of Scope

- Sharing a watchlist between profiles.
