# Curated Collections

## Intent

A catalogue of four thousand films is too many to browse. Editors pick films that belong together and say why, so a viewer finds a film the way they would in a good video shop.

## Open Questions

- Who may publish a collection: any editor, or only the lead editor?

## Out of Scope

- Collections made by viewers.
