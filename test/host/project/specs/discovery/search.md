# Search

## Intent

A viewer who knows what they want should find it in a few letters, whether they remember the title, the director, or an actor.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** Results update with each letter typed.
- **REQ2:** A film's original title finds it as well as its English one.

### Performance

- **REQ3:** Results arrive in under a second on the TV.

## Out of Scope

- Searching inside subtitles.
