# Offline Downloads

## Intent

Commuters lose their signal underground and on planes. A viewer should be able to save a film at home on Wi-Fi and watch it anywhere, for as long as the licence for that film allows.

## Open Questions

- How many films may one profile keep downloaded at once?

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** A film with a download licence shows a Download button with its size.
- **REQ2:** A downloaded film plays with no connection until its licence ends.

## Out of Scope

- Downloads on the TV and the web.
