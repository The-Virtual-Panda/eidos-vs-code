# Subtitles and Audio

## Intent

A viewer who watches with French subtitles should never have to turn them on again. The choice belongs to the profile, and follows it to every film that has that language.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** A film starts with the subtitle and audio language the profile chose last.
- **REQ2:** When a film lacks that language, it starts with the film's original audio and no subtitles.

## Out of Scope

- Subtitle styling beyond the size setting.
