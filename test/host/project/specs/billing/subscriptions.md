# Subscriptions

## Intent

A viewer pays monthly or yearly, tries the service free for a week first, and leaves without calling anyone. A cancellation that takes two taps keeps more people than one that hides.

## Requirements

### Functional

- **REQ1:** A new account starts a free week, and is charged on the eighth day unless it cancels.
- **REQ2:** Cancel is two taps from Account, on every device.

## Out of Scope

- Gift subscriptions.
