# Gift Cards

## Intent

A viewer buys a month or a year of Reelhouse for someone else, and the person who receives it starts watching without entering a card.


## Requirements

### Functional

* **REQ1:** A gift card is bought for one, three, or twelve months.
- **REQ2:** The person who redeems it starts with no card on file.
